How to Use OmniAI Platform Step-by-Step Guide
1

Unlock Platform & Credentials

The platform is locked with password vimalraj45. Your Cloudflare credentials (Account ID & Token) are loaded automatically from the server's .env file. If you ever want to use a different Cloudflare account, simply open Settings and enter your custom keys.

2

Selecting the Right Model for Your Task

• Daily Chat & General QA: Use llama-3.1-8b (fast and saves neurons).
• Deep Reasoning & Logic: Use deepseek-r1-distill-qwen-32b or gpt-oss-120b.
• Coding & Programming: Use qwen2.5-coder-32b-instruct for complete code snippets and refactoring.
• Image Generation: Use flux-1-schnell for hyper-realistic renders or sdxl-lightning for fast generation.
• Image QA & Vision: Use llava-1.5-7b to upload an image and ask questions about it.
• Audio Speech-to-Text: Upload audio in the Media tab to transcribe with whisper.

3

Tracking & Maximizing Your 10,000 Daily Free Neurons

Cloudflare gives you 10,000 Neurons every single day for free (resets automatically at 00:00 UTC). The sidebar and topbar live meters estimate your usage after every request so you never run out of quota.

4

Hosting Free on Render

You can deploy this entire repository to Render for free! Simply push this repo to GitHub, link it in Render Dashboard, set CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID, CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN, and APP_PASSWORD in Render Environment Variables, and your personal AI platform is live 24/7.
